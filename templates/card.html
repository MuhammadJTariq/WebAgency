Step 01

Discovery

Before a single line of code gets written or a template gets chosen, we sit down and talk. Discovery is the foundation of every project I take on — and it's the part most web builders skip.

In this phase, I take time to understand who you are, what your business does, and what you actually need your website to accomplish. That last part matters most. A website isn't just a digital brochure — it's a working tool, and it should be built around a clear purpose.

We'll talk through your audience, your goals, your competitors, and any sites you admire. I'll ask questions you might not have thought about yet — and that's the point. Discovery often helps clients realize what they truly need is different from what they originally asked for, and that clarity saves time, money, and frustration down the line.
